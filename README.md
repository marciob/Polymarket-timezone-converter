# Prediction Market Timezone Converter

Prediction Market Timezone Converter is a powerful Chrome extension designed to enhance the trading experience across **Polymarket** prediction market. It automatically converts event deadlines and settlement times from platform defaults (e.g., ET/UTC) to your local timezone.

The extension seamlessly integrates into the interface of supported platforms, displaying converted times alongside the originals for time-sensitive markets—including 15-minute, hourly, and daily events—ensuring you never miss a critical trade window. With a sleek, dark-themed popup interface, you can easily customize your preferred timezone or rely on automatic detection, while intelligent scanning ensures accuracy without cluttering the UI.

## Features

- **Automatic Conversion**: Instantly converts ET times to your local timezone.
- **Smart Integration**: Displays converted times next to the original text.
- **Support for All Durations**: Works with 15-min, hourly, and 4-hour markets.
- **Customizable**: Choose your preferred timezone or use auto-detection.
- **Premium UI**: Dark-themed, glassmorphic settings popup.

## Installation

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the extension folder.

## Usage

1. Open a market page on [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com).
2. Look for the green timezone conversion next to market times.
   - Example: `January 7, 7-8PM ET (BRT: 9:00 PM - 10:00 PM)`
3. Click the extension icon to change your timezone settings.
