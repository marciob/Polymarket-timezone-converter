# Privacy Policy for Prediction Market Timezone Converter

## Data Collection
Prediction Market Timezone Converter ("the Extension") does not collect, transmit, or sell any user data.

## Permissions
- **Storage**: The Extension uses the local chrome storage API solely to save your timezone preference setting. This data never leaves your browser.
- **Host Permissions**: The Extension accesses `polymarket.com` solely to read the visible text on the page for the purpose of time conversion. It does not read your account details, trades, or financial information.

## Third-Party Services
The Extension operates entirely client-side and does not communicate with any third-party servers.
