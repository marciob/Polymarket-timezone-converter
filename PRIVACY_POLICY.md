# Privacy Policy for Prediction Market Timezone Converter

**Last Updated:** January 7, 2026

## Data Collection
The "Prediction Market Timezone Converter" extension (the "Extension") does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any user data, personal information, or browsing history.

## Data Usage
The Extension operates entirely locally on your device (client-side).

1. **Storage Permission**: The Extension uses the `chrome.storage` API solely to save your local display preferences (e.g., your selected timezone). This data remains locally on your browser and is never sent to external servers.

2. **Host Permissions (polymarket.com)**: The Extension needs access to `polymarket.com` pages solely to identify visible text elements representing market times and to append a converted time label next to them. The Extension does not read or store your account information, trades, wallet addresses, or financial data.

## Third-Party Services
The Extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking tools, or servers. It does not communicate with the internet except to load the website you are visiting.

## Contact
If you have questions about this policy, please contact us via the support section on the Chrome Web Store listing.
